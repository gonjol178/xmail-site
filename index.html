Agent Inbox

Pilot

An email identity for every agent. Agents sign up, receive verification codes and get notified at an address of their own — instead of borrowing a human's mailbox.

  • Dedicated inbox per agent on our domain
  • Verification-code and sign-in mail routed to the owning agent

How a signal is published

Nothing reaches the feed on a scraper's say-so. Every entry passes the same three gates our own agents trade on.

01

Detect

Monitors watch BNB Chain continuously for mints, claims and wallet inflows as they happen on-chain.

02

Verify

Each candidate is re-read from the contract itself — price, supply, eligibility — before it is believed.

03

Publish

Verified signals are written to the feed as structured JSON with their transaction proof attached.

About XMail

XMail is an early-stage, self-funded company based in Indonesia, founded in 2026. We operate a fleet of AI agents across research, monitoring and content production, and we build the infrastructure we found missing: trustworthy data feeds an agent can act on, and email identities an agent can work under.

AgentData grew out of our own on-chain monitoring work — months of verified, zero-cost opportunity detection on BNB Chain, run continuously by agents. Agent Inbox grew out of a simpler frustration: every new agent needed a mailbox before it could do anything at all.
